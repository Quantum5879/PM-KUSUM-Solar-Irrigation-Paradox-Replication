Do Subsidised Solar Pumps Really Cut Carbon or Shift the Cost to Groundwater?
Evidence from India's PM-KUSUM
Harsh Dagar and Gunjan Bhandari
Division of Dairy Economics, Statistics and Management
ICAR-National Dairy Research Institute (Deemed University), Karnal, Haryana 132001, India

This Supplementary Information accompanies the main article. It contains Supplementary Tables S1-S3 (carbon sensitivity, IV diagnostics, and social-cost ledger) and Supplementary Figures S1-S10 (causal pathway, geographic alignment, event study, paradox scales, component identification, heterogeneous effects, rebound sensitivity, leave-one-out, carbon balance, and governance matrix). Figures are rendered at 400 dpi. Figures 1-4 in the main text correspond to executive visuals V2, V4, V5 and V9.
Supplementary Tables
Table S1. Carbon sensitivity matrix
Gross, rebound and net abatement (Mt CO2/yr) across diesel displacement, pump energy intensity, lift depth and grid emission factor. is_central = 1 marks the paper's central bridge case (600 L, e = 0.004, lift = 20 m, EF = 0.7383).


Table S2. IV diagnostics
Instrumental-variable specifications for groundwater stage and related outcomes. Weak_IV_flag marks first-stage F < 10. The final row summarises leave-one-out sign stability.


Table S3. Social cost ledger
Gross abatement value, rebound cost, net value and over-claim cost (INR crore) at alternative social cost of carbon (INR per tCO2). Zone rows use SCC = 1,500 INR/t.
